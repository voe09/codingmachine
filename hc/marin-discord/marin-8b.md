## Discussions and learnings

- The initial 8B model trailed Llama 3.1 8B on MMLU and instruct evaluations, but successive cooldowns and SFT improved it. By early May the base model was reported slightly ahead on MMLU, while AlpacaEval remained sensitive to the post-training recipe. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1355318614802042950/1369391292500672583).
- Participants cautioned that a small preference benchmark can saturate and that knowledge gains alone may not raise AlpacaEval wins without preference optimization. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1355318614802042950/1368019067310444598).
