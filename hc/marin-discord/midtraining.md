## Discussions and learnings

- Midtraining experiments tested Nemotron math data on the Delphi scaling ladder, varying learning rate, mixture, warmup, and token budget. Evaluation contamination mattered: a later check found low GSM8K 10-gram overlap but serious MATH-500 overlap in Nemotron CC-math. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1523154815599902791).
- The channel noted that stale or unexplained evaluations make midtraining conclusions hard to compare; deduplication and up-to-date experiment records were part of the lesson. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1521407119423836262).
