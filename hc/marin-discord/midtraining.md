## Math continued pretraining on the Delphi ladder

The central experiment took fully annealed Delphi checkpoints and continued training on Nemotron CC-math mixed with replayed pretraining data. The team called this “midtraining” but noted it was closer to continued pretraining because the source checkpoints had completed annealing. For 1e20, 1e21, and 1e22-FLOP IsoFLOP models, the new-token budget was 20% of pretraining tokens. Sweeps varied pretraining/math ratios of 67/33, 50/50, and 33/67 and learning-rate multipliers from 0.33× to 0.83×. Math validation loss improved most in the high-math, lower-learning-rate region while more pretraining replay better preserved general Paloma loss. Perplexity was treated as a noisy proxy for downstream math ability, not the final objective. [Sweep setup and tradeoff](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1501637620588089424).

Forecasting this phase was harder than forecasting a stable pretraining recipe. In one analysis, a within-cell curve needed about half of a run to predict its final validation loss within 0.02 nats; pooling across learning rates or mix settings did not improve that fit. Extrapolating from smaller runs to 1e22 with a Chinchilla-style asymptote missed by about 0.1 nats, versus roughly 2.5–3% error for 1e21. One 1e22 group also accidentally used 3.4% warmup rather than the intended 10%, a reminder that apparently small configuration differences can invalidate a scaling comparison. [Forecast analysis](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1507658133844263036).

The end-to-end follow-up took midtrained checkpoints through hyperparameter search, SFT, and Evalchemy. MATH-500 reportedly rose from about 2 to 45 across the 3e18→1e22 ladder in the best cells, with the 33% replay/67% math mix generally ranking above more replay-heavy mixes. The post-training “cold-start” benefit was larger at small scale and narrowed toward the largest tested model. The authors cautioned that Delphi models were not overtrained and had little math in their original pretraining mix; comparisons with external models therefore needed FLOP and recipe context. [SFT/eval report](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1517650624915836928).

## Evaluation validity and production handoff

A later 10-gram overlap audit found GSM8K overlap of 2.43% of test and 1.95% of train against Nemotron CC-math, but approximately 28% of MATH-500 examples overlapped. Manual inspection found source documents containing both question and solution, making MATH-500 gains particularly hard to interpret as uncontaminated generalization. The team explicitly called for deduplication and clearer, up-to-date experiment records; stale scores should not silently become headline results. [Contamination audit](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1523154815599902791) · [Record caveat](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1521407119423836262).

The Snowball 67B-A2B cooldown later provided an operational base for the full post-training chain: chat templating, thinking/SFT experiments, vLLM serving, and evaluations ran end to end. A reported 700,000-example SFT took under an hour and evals around 30 minutes; dense-mixer support for router adjustment remained a caveat. This establishes a reusable pipeline, not by itself a downstream-quality verdict. [Snowball handoff](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1483266366772351067/1527726088799649953).
