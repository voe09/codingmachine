## Discussions and learnings

- Inference work covered vLLM integration, reproducible evaluation, multi-host serving, and numerical differences between training and serving. Under one controlled comparison, vLLM delivered about 2.6x Levanter throughput. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1385733711013871729/1427163698522488832).
- Trainer-versus-server differences were traced partly to ragged paged attention and a layer-fold implementation, motivating parity checks before attributing quality changes to weights. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1385733711013871729/1437252801750106185).
