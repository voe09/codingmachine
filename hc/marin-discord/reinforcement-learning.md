# #reinforcement-learning

Archive coverage: 2025-05-22–2026-09-23 (UTC); 1,852 messages, including 1,010 thread replies. [Open channel in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1374989195109466122).

## Discussion and lessons

- RL discussion moved from algorithm trade-offs and vLLM rollout integration to stable Iris/Fray training and post-training Snowball models. A key systems result was that replacing a failing Ray execution path with on-demand workers allowed a 500-step stability test. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1486914443060449320).
- Early algorithm discussion distinguished synchronous GRPO-style clipping, asynchronous truncated importance sampling, and KL-regularized policy improvement. The recommendation to support the first two was a design choice, not a proof that one wins on Marin tasks. [Discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1385442628694183996).
- Online RL depended on reliable rollouts and serving; vLLM was integrated into the training pipeline in 2025. Later migration from Ray to on-demand Iris/Fray workers isolated execution reliability from algorithm performance. [Discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1430965818241454254).
- A Snowball RL run improved AIME24 from 17.67% to 27%, MATH-500 from 64% to 78%, and OlympiadBench from 12.67% to 20% under the reported setup. The report also observed reasoning changes, though subsequent agent-task transfer had to be evaluated separately. [Discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1542681739497832499).
- A later agent-RL stack lifted held-out r2egym pass@1 from 50.1% to 59.8%, but the comparison included concurrent Harbor, tooling, and data fixes. A separate Snowball run roughly doubled SWE-Bench pass@1 after r2egym RL without improving Terminal-Bench 2. A rising training reward is therefore not enough to establish broad agent capability. [Stack comparison](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1549558259713703986); [transfer test](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1550202720433086504).
- A follow-up reported that SFT followed by RL added more than eight SWE-Bench points over SFT or RL alone in its Snowball setup. This suggests testing the handoff between supervised and online training, not treating them as competing recipes. [Discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1374989195109466122/1552048522403250178).
