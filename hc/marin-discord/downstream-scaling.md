# #downstream-scaling

Archive coverage: 2026-04-14–2026-05-19 (UTC); 31 messages, including 9 thread replies. Every exported message body was reviewed chronologically. [Open channel in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1493722964644990996).

The proxy-evaluation project asked whether a cheaper signal could predict the response of a much larger model to the *same* mid- or post-training intervention. The proposed experiment was to run a ladder of model sizes through matched data mixes, then see whether changes measured on smaller models forecast the largest model's outcome. Participants suggested current Terminal-Bench, SWE-bench, and SWE mid-training datasets as candidate interventions, but the channel does not report completed matched-ladder results for them. This design is stronger than merely showing that better base models score better: it asks whether the *effect of a treatment* transfers across scale. [Project scope](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1493722964644990996/1495899857578103014) · [Candidate mixes](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1495899857578103014/1495901481830842459) · [Proxy-noise concern](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1493722964644990996/1495920933116968970).

Even fitting a simpler extrapolation required care. A first GSM8K trace-loss fit from models below 1e21 compute to 1e23 was off by roughly 0.1 nats and systematically predicted worse outcomes than observed. The author noted the estimate averaged only 256 problems and that trace loss might not scale as smoothly as generic language-model loss. In comparing with a Delphi blog figure, another participant pointed out that an asymptote term was needed and had been estimated using non-Marin public models. The thread did not establish that the missing asymptote fully explained the residual; it established a plausible model-specification check before rejecting scaling as a proxy. [Initial residual and sample caveat](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1493722964644990996/1506401125471879298) · [Direction of error](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1493722964644990996/1506401284016701632) · [Asymptote suggestion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1506402137117032532/1506404986765049956) · [Public-model estimate](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1506402137117032532/1506405099369660517).

A second approach made GSM8K deliberately easier by randomly masking tokens in a worked solution, varying the mask probability. The early curves could often extrapolate the 1e23 model from 3e20-and-smaller models, but showed an uptick at the small-model end—the part needed for cheap prediction. Other knobs, such as best-of-N decoding with a stronger model, appeared to avoid that uptick in preliminary checks, but their evaluation runs were not finished in the archive. Thus “promising” meant candidate proxy curves worth stress-testing across multiple ladders, not a validated replacement for full-scale benchmarks.
