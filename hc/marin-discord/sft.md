## Discussions and learnings

- SFT work compared reasoning datasets and base models, including OpenThoughts training on long-context Marin 8B versus Qwen. Initial AIME results favored Qwen substantially; later messages revisited intermediate checkpoints and repeated trials rather than treating one endpoint as definitive. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1355318637854199848/1458203611543900190).
- The channel also treated format normalization, special tokens, data contamination, and model stability as necessary parts of post-training—not just dataset volume. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1355318637854199848/1465858152133169182).
