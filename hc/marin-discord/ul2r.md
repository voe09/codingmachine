## Objective implementation and debugging

UL2R work tried to add denoising and prefix-style objectives to a pretrained GPT-2 path. The main difficulty was not launching a run; it was proving that the special tokens, loss masks, and attention matrices represented the intended tasks. An early bug produced gibberish. After fixing that, the plan was to inspect masks, test whether a small model understood the task tokens, and only then run a training/evaluation experiment. Later mask fixes made samples look plausible but surfaced NaNs or a lower-level attention error, so visual inspection alone was insufficient. [Early debugging plan](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1409936223509811272/1427861705228030133) · [Attention error](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1409936223509811272/1433320112265363487).

Interpreting downstream evaluations remained unsettled. The group considered whether PrefixLM bidirectional masks also belonged in evaluation, whether the GPT-2 probe was too weak for a small expected QA improvement to rise above noise, and whether more evaluation examples were needed. A later comparison found that WikiText training examples included a beginning-of-sequence token absent in HellaSwag and that training versus HellaSwag loss/attention masks were shifted by one token. Those mismatches could invalidate an apparent negative result before any conclusion about UL2R itself. [Evaluation hypotheses](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1409936223509811272/1440999846810095656) · [Token and mask mismatch](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1409936223509811272/1446052753523740672).

The archive does not establish a reliable downstream improvement from this implementation. Its most reusable lesson is methodological: construct tiny examples with known visible-token and target-token positions, compare training and evaluation formatting, then inspect logits and raw outputs before trusting aggregate benchmark numbers. [High initial-loss concern](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1409936223509811272/1446010126317391907).
