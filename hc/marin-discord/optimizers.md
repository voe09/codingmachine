## Discussions and learnings

- Optimizer discussion compared Adam-family variants, Muon, PSGD, and hyperball updates across Speedrun and larger Marin runs. The group repeatedly distinguished normalized-update learning-rate semantics from Adam's and watched precision/sharding effects. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1382240679765217342/1517016423119978496).
- An apparent bfloat16-parameter regression in a hyperball setup was treated as an unresolved precision/rounding hypothesis rather than evidence that the optimizer cannot work on TPU. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1382240679765217342/1517017643952111737).
