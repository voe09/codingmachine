- GPU work measured MoE training efficiency and optimizer/sharding cost. - A later PGLE experiment reached about 25 MFU on a 360B-A23B configuration, underscoring that hardware and kernel improvements can change the earlier trade-off. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1399998407657001062/1530411117279711383).
