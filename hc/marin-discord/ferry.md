- Ferry runs were proposed as periodic 1B/8B integration checks, with Speedrun as a gate. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1443338055728496824/1446216653724455104).
- The promotion path was explicit: test a single idea cheaply in Speedrun, then include promising changes in periodic 1B/8B Ferry runs to assess combined effects. A larger-scale effect need not match the smallest ablation. [Discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1443338055728496824/1446218478817247493).
