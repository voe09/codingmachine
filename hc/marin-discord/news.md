Archive coverage: 2025-04-29–2026-09-22 (UTC); 1,349 messages, including 631 thread replies. [Open channel in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1356487690559684638).

## Discussions and learnings

- The news channel is a research and release feed: members shared external papers, model launches, reports, and event announcements, often with brief discussion of relevance to Marin. It should not be read as a record of adopted Marin decisions. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1356487690559684638/1445303409258397860).
- OpenThoughts-Next's kickoff exemplified how a news post can lead to a dedicated project channel for detailed work. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1356487690559684638/1521186176743112795).
