Archive coverage: 2025-07-02–2026-02-25 (UTC); 305 messages, including 2 thread replies. [Open channel in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1390096969858547823).

## Discussions and learnings

- The multilingual 8B effort sequenced language/eval selection, corpus selection, training, then re-evaluation. Contributors warned that transfer depends on script sharing and the balance of high- and low-resource languages, so a fixed per-language token threshold is only a rough heuristic. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1390847766703505519).
- The channel assembled an evaluation spreadsheet and adapted a prior 8B cooldown experiment as a starting point; the cited messages describe process more than a final multilingual model result. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1394706082189807866).
