## Designing the continued-pretraining run

The multilingual 8B effort began by defining evaluation languages and benchmarks before choosing a corpus or launching training. FineWeb 2 was a default candidate, but the group wanted a per-language comparison against existing models rather than assuming a corpus with broad language labels would produce broad capability. Contributors warned that transfer depends on script sharing and the balance of high- and low-resource languages; a fixed token quota per language is only a rough heuristic. The benchmark list also sought to avoid machine-translated or LLM-generated tests without human validation. [Workflow proposal](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1394706082189807866) · [Transfer caveat](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1390847766703505519) · [Benchmark filter](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1394757220767371364).

The proposed implementation adapted an existing 8B training script, with a short rewarmup, a long multilingual continued-pretraining phase, and a possible balance between Nemotron English/code data and added multilingual data. The precise mixture was debated; one suggestion put 70% on Nemotron and 30% on new multilingual text. The project was framed as a community-influenced run rather than a fixed top-down recipe. [Initial training plan](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1399620235144728696) · [Mixture discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1433718953389195344).

## Measurement and outcome limits

Contributors added or proposed LM Evaluation Harness tasks including LM-SynEval, ZhoBLiMP, TurBLiMP, and BLiMP-NL. The continued-pretraining run started by late October, and a base checkpoint was later published. Early macro losses looked healthy but largely omitted non-English data, so they could not establish multilingual improvement. Initial distribution-shift spikes smoothed out. Later inference comparisons included Marin's multilingual CPT checkpoint, Llama, and Qwen; the group explicitly requested the original monolingual English Marin model as a baseline to measure the *incremental* effect of CPT. [Eval ports](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1403372165549654131) · [Training status](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1433605433599197295) · [Loss caveat](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1435021645860307029) · [Checkpoint](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1437941799279919184) · [Baseline request](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1390096969858547823/1458129832499085555).

The archive supports that a multilingual CPT model was trained and evaluated, but the cited channel discussion does not establish a single aggregate multilingual win across all languages and tasks. Continued pretraining from an English-oriented base is also not equivalent to multilingual pretraining from scratch.
