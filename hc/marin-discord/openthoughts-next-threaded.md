## Discussions and learnings

- A [pinned project-scope decision](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1534221005038555227) assigned evaluation baselines to the PIs and experiment plans to the workstream leads. It required permissively licensed teacher models, selected MarinSkyRL for RL, made Marin models the primary target, and reserved Qwen models for ablations. It named the RL, evaluation, data, and midtraining leads and specified a PI vote for adding new leads.
- The forum coordinated agentic-evaluation mechanics: an OpenThoughts-Agent launcher guide pointed to [Marin issue #6877](https://github.com/marin-community/marin/issues/6877), a separate post flagged a leaderboard outage, and another requested evaluations of the first Qwen3 Coder 30B-A3B RL data-ablation cohort. These are operations and evaluation requests, not a final model-quality claim.
- Research pointers included LLM-as-verifier methods, Lightning on-policy distillation, multi-teacher on-policy distillation, and a pre/post-training scaling paper. The posts framed these as candidates to evaluate; they did not establish a change to the agreed recipe.
- A tooling post recommended mumwelt and marinmirror for searching Marin code and public research context. A short cross-post directed ongoing discussion back to `#openthoughts-next`.
