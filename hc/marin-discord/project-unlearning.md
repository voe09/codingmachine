## Define a failure before claiming unlearning works

The proposed starting experiment was to train paired ~1B models with and without documents about a target concept, then test whether the filtered model still produced verbatim passages. The group immediately questioned whether some published “lingering sequence” examples demonstrated memorization at all: an LLM can copy a prompt or extend a trivial pattern without having learned the excluded work. A convincing failure would have to show generation of substantive withheld text despite its exclusion, with controls against prompt leakage and generic completions. The channel describes a planned speedrun and benchmark design, not a completed Marin unlearning result. [Proposed paired runs](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1388757310930686004/1390500946009919621) · [Weak-failure concern](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1388757310930686004/1391838691571535892) · [Desired failure mode](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1388757310930686004/1391840374364049418).

The benchmark discussion also separated **privacy, copyright, and safety**. Each asks for a different notion of what must become inaccessible and a different comparison baseline; a fact artificially inserted by fine-tuning, as in some TOFU-style setups, may be a weak proxy for knowledge acquired during pretraining. Participants wanted to avoid treating these application areas as one undifferentiated “unlearning” score. [Application-specific baselines](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1395331282560356533/1395417567631511622) · [Definitions should not be conflated](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1395331282560356533/1395419083167760395) · [TOFU skepticism](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1395331282560356533/1395418969284018312).

An external researcher described a planned DCLM pair differing by a WMDP-like filter and reported that pretraining filtering resisted adversarial fine-tuning better than two post-training circuit-breaking variants in their study. The discussion corrected an initial description of the source data as science-focused; it was DCLM. The actual plot values are not in the exported text, and the external study was not independently analyzed here, so this is a reported comparison rather than a numeric Marin finding. It informed the group's interest in baselines that test whether a removed capability can be relearned under an adversarial intervention.
