## Discussion and lessons

- Early Marin-8B preference-tuning experiments released DPO and ORPO checkpoints. In a reported three-shot GSM8K comparison, Marin 8B Instruct scored 0.56, an early DPO stage 0.67, an early ORPO stage 0.61, and DPO stage 2 reached 0.70. The authors also warned that unconventional dataset choices left some checkpoints failing to emit the end-of-sequence token. A benchmark gain therefore did not establish usable generation behavior; basic stop-condition and length tests belonged next to task scores.
