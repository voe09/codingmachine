# #rlhf

Archive coverage: 2025-05-22–2025-06-19 (UTC); 32 messages, including 2 thread replies. Every exported message body and reply in this channel was reviewed. [Open channel in Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1354881461060243556/1375176346287739051).

The channel's substantive post-training experiment was preference optimization of Marin-8B-Instruct, with DPO and ORPO checkpoints released in stages. On a reported three-shot GSM8K evaluation, the original instruct checkpoint scored **0.56**, DPO stage 0 **0.67**, ORPO stage 0 **0.61**, and DPO stage 2 **0.70**. An AlpacaEval run was also reported at **24.83** for a DPO checkpoint versus **21** for the instruct comparison, but the evaluator had not yet inspected its generations. These are checkpoint- and harness-specific reports, not an isolated comparison of DPO against ORPO on identical data and compute. [Stage results](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1375176346287739051/1376623490584674384) · [AlpacaEval caveat](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1375176346287739051/1376624180098764850).

The most important caveat was operational: unconventional preference-data choices left some released checkpoints often failing to emit the end-of-sequence token. This could distort generation length, usability, and perhaps an instruction-following evaluation despite better task scores. Participants connected the issue to observed AlpacaEval behavior and requested trying a different preference mix; the export records a plan to train on it, not that the EOS defect was fixed. The named data sources for the first DPO runs were `mlabonne/orpo-dpo-mix-40k` and `orpo-explorers/OpenHermesPreferences-10k`. This illustrates why a preference-tuning result needs generation inspection and stop-condition checks alongside benchmark accuracy. [EOS warning](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1375176346287739051/1376623490584674384) · [Data sources](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1375176346287739051/1376624778315698286) · [Alternative requested](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1375176346287739051/1376627620086808657) · [Training intent](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1375176346287739051/1377267207708606545).

Releasing the checkpoints in a format the evaluation stack could consume was another dependency: the group requested Safetensors exports from EasyDeL, and an ORPO Torch/FP32 version was later uploaded. The remaining June messages were a speculative discussion of JAX serving engines, not another RLHF experiment.
