## Why deduplication order matters

Deduplication is not just a storage-saving preprocessing step; it changes the effective training distribution. One concern was that deduplicating *after* combining curated sources could remove repeat appearances of especially good documents selected by several high-quality datasets, unintentionally reducing their weight. The group therefore needed to reason about where dedup sits relative to quality selection and mixture construction. It also discussed loose duplicates common in synthetic data: when token supply is ample, dropping them may be reasonable, but the similarity rule must reflect the desired sampling policy. [Mixture concern](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1441211384279994529/1443154063263928351) · [Loose synthetic duplicates](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1508892390037917888/1511249417150271489).

The pipeline's scale made reproducibility and systems behavior inseparable. A May 2026 run processed 15,089,658,952 documents. About 68.33% had no detected duplicate, 31.67% belonged to a duplicate cluster, and retaining one canonical per cluster would keep 77.75% overall while removing 22.25%. Those are counts under that algorithm and corpus, not a general duplicate rate for web data. Shared-memory sizing, remote file access, and cross-dataset comparison all mattered at this scale. [Run counts](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1441211384279994529/1508887247796178985) · [Resource discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1441211384279994529/1445558614734409809).

When new data arrives, an incremental dedup pass is not necessarily simpler than rerunning from scratch: cross-dataset duplicates still require comparing all documents, and a fresh pass keeps the processing graph easier to reproduce unless overlap is extreme. Later discussions questioned whether semantic near-duplicates can undermine scaling-law estimates even after lexical dedup, shifting the research question from pipeline speed to the effect of redundancy on model learning. [Incremental tradeoff](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1463590986910404660/1463608037850742784) · [Semantic-duplicate question](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1501300822502412288/1540268761624481882).
