## Discussion and lessons

- The group also proposed using differently specialized Datashop models as MoE experts; this was a hypothesis about avoiding copied-expert under-specialization, not a demonstrated result here. [Source](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1356484955852509245/1374422088043073686).
- In a medical anneal, MedMCQA rose from 0.386 to 0.452 and HeadQA from 0.359 to 0.391, while PubMedQA barely changed (0.754 to 0.758). A specialized corpus did not transfer evenly across medical benchmarks. [Discussion](https://marin-discord.pages.dev/#1356484955852509245/1379684718089343028).
